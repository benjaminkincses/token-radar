01 / The methodology

How the value is built

TVL × category multiple × growth × maturity × breadth

Category multiples reflect tokenized peers per dollar of TVL. Growth uses the 7-day change, clamped to −50%…150%. Listing age shapes maturity; chain count shapes breadth. The low–high band is 0.5× to 1.8× implied value.

Bridges, wrappers, exchange products and versioned duplicates are excluded. These are model estimates, not market prices.
